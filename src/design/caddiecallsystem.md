# Caddie Call System

Ordered best → worst:

1. Attack
2. Play
3. Manage
4. Careful
5. Liability

Fallback:
- Insufficient Data

## Definitions

Attack:
- Strong trust
- Lean in

Play:
- Reliable
- Go-to option

Manage:
- Usable, but requires awareness

Careful:
- Risk present
- Avoid aggressive play

Liability:
- Avoid
- High risk outcome

## Rules
- Labels are action-oriented
- Never describe, always guide
- Must feel like a real caddie
