# Caddie Call System
## The Looper Call System

The product uses two core outputs:

- Score (0–100)
- Call

These replace previous references to:
- Caddie Score
- Caddie Call

All UI, labels, and copy should use:
- Score
- Call

Call Ordered best → worst:

1. Attack
2. Play
3. Manage
4. Careful
5. Liability

Fallback:
- Insufficient Data

## Definitions

Attack:
- Strong trust
- Lean in

Play:
- Reliable
- Go-to option

Manage:
- Usable, but requires awareness

Careful:
- Risk present
- Avoid aggressive play

Liability:
- Avoid
- High risk outcome; consider other playable options

## Rules
- Labels are action-oriented
- Never describe, always guide
- Must feel like a real caddie who knows your game
