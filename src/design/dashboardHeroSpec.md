This file defines the top spotlight band of the Dashboard.

This is the most premium section of the page.
It must feel more editorial and atmospheric than the club grid below.

## Structure

The hero section contains exactly two cards:

1. Spotlight on Your Game
2. Trend to Watch

They appear side-by-side on desktop.

## Card 1: Spotlight on Your Game

Required content:
- eyebrow: Spotlight on Your Game
- headline: primary overall takeaway
- 2–3 bullets
- supporting icon
- subtle course / flag / green texture atmosphere

Intent:
- should feel like the strongest editorial conclusion on the page
- should immediately tell the user where their game stands

## Card 2: Trend to Watch

Required content:
- eyebrow: Trend to Watch
- headline: recent movement or cross-club pattern
- 2–3 bullets
- supporting icon
- subtle gold flourish or differentiated atmosphere

Intent:
- should help the user know what is changing
- should feel connected to the first card, but slightly lighter in emphasis

## Visual Rules

- dark green layered surface
- white-first text hierarchy
- gold accent only
- premium but restrained
- subtle background imagery allowed
- no admin-style controls in this area
- no undo/export actions here

## Not Allowed

- plain black hero blocks
- generic dashboard widgets
- raw metric overload
- utility-first styling
- bright stock-photo imagery
