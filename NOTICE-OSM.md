## Greywolf proof fixture

The Greywolf Hole 1 proof uses a static extract from the bounding box south 50.43, west -116.28, north 50.47, east -116.22. The registration proof retained OSM tee element 1209190795 for Hole 1. The original proof did not retain the exact fetch time, OSM base timestamp, or every surface element ID. Future generated course fixtures must retain those fields in their provenance metadata.
